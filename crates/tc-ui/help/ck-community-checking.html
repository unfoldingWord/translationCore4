Community Checking preview

Read the whole book as a printed page, set up the page, and export a copy for reviewers.

Use it to read the book as a whole, or to read it aloud with your community.

Preview and export

  1. Click Check, then Open → on the Community Checking card.
  2. Scroll the preview. Verses that are not drafted yet show as "[ verse not yet drafted ]".
  3. In Page setup, choose Columns (1 or 2), Spacing (Single or Double) and Paper size (A4 or Letter). Turn Drop-cap chapters and Verse numbers on or off.
  4. Click Export to save a copy. See Export a book as PDF, Export a book as USFM and Export a project as a Scripture Burrito.

To go back, click ← All checking tools.

Good to know

  • Double spacing leaves room for reviewers to write between the lines.
  • The page setup changes only the preview and the export, not your translation.
  • With Drop-cap chapters on, the large chapter number starts verse 1, so verse 1 has no small number of its own. Turn drop caps off to show it.
  • translationCore does not keep the page setup when you leave this screen. Set it again before you export.
  • Footnotes is greyed out and cannot be turned on yet.
