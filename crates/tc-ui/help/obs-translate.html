Translate a story

Draft the story title, each frame and the reference line, with the picture and the English story beside you.

Stories are drafted part by part: the title, each frame, then the reference.

Steps

  1. Open a story and click Translate.
  2. Click Draft the title (1) and type the title.
  1. Click Save.
  2. For each frame, click "Draft frame N" (2), look at the picture and the English text, and type your translation. Click Save (1).
  1. At the end, click Draft the reference and translate the line "A Bible story from: …".

To change a saved part, click its text. To cancel, click Cancel.

Good to know

  • The story's percent drafted on the left goes up as you save parts.
  • Notes, words and questions for the frame are in the helps panel on the right. They stay on that frame after you click Save. The Comments tab shows the newest comment of that frame.
  • The frame box is as tall as the picture and text beside it. Longer text scrolls inside the box; drag its corner to make it taller.
  • Stories have no verse numbers or spans.
