The helps panel in Translate

While you draft, the helps panel shows the notes, word links, questions, simplified text and your own comments for the verses in focus. Notes are grouped under section and verse headings, and word cards open with the first paragraph of their article.

It sits on the right of Translate, next to your draft.

Steps

  1. If the panel is hidden, click Toggle helps panel at the top right of the chapter.
  2. Click a tab.
  1. Open a section or verse editor. The panel narrows to the verses you are editing.

The tabs

  • Notes: translation notes, grouped under uppercase headings such as INTRODUCTION and VERSE 1. Each note that has a Translation Academy article links by the article's real name (for example Abstract Nouns →), not a generic label.
  • Words: key terms. Each card shows the first paragraph of the Translation Words article and Read the article →. Click a card to highlight every occurrence of that word in the section.
  • Questions: questions with answers, to test your draft's meaning.
  • UST: the simplified text of the chapter. Some gateway languages give this tab another name, such as GST.
  • Comments: comments you wrote in Understand. It says "Nothing in this chapter." when there are none.

Good to know

  • The helps follow what you clicked: a section in Section mode, a verse in Verse mode, or a story frame. They stay on it after you click Save, until you click another one or leave the chapter.
  • Point at a Notes card to highlight its phrase in the source; click it to keep the highlight.
  • Click a Words card to highlight every matching word across the section you are reading.
  • Long notes have Show more and Show less.
  • Drag the vertical divider between your draft and the helps to make the panel wider or narrower. Toggle helps panel shows or hides the panel completely (one button; there is no separate widen control).
  • Helps articles open with a floating close control above a divider, and render line breaks, superscripts and underlines from the source cleanly.
  • Each help is explained in the Understand section.
