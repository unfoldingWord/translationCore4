Find a story on Home

The stories card shows three stories. Show all 50 to pick any story.

Each story is a tile, like a book tile in a Bible project. It shows the story number, title and percent drafted.

Steps

  1. On Home, find your stories card. It has the OBS badge.
  2. Click a tile to open that story, or click Show all 50 stories → (1).
  1. Click any story in the grid.

To fold the grid again, click Show recent stories only → at the bottom of the card.

Good to know

  • Resume → at the top of Home reopens the last story you edited.
  • After a sync that finds frame conflicts, a story card can show an amber 1 conflict · Review chip. Open Review to resolve each frame.
