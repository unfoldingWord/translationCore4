Editing a verse you already checked or aligned

When you change a verse that has check decisions or alignments, tC4 flags them for review. Nothing is deleted.

Checks and alignments are tied to the exact words of a verse, so when those words change tC4 marks what may no longer fit.

Steps

  1. Change a verse in Translate that you already checked or aligned.
  2. Go to Check and open the checking tool. A notice at the top says how many decisions need review.
  1. Click the Invalid filter to see those items, and decide each one again.
  2. Open Align for the verse. Words you changed are back in the word bank. Place them and click ✓ Mark valid again.

Good to know

  • A check decision whose words changed becomes Invalid. It is kept, not deleted.
  • In Align, links to the words you didn't change are kept. The verse shows as to do again.
  • A link stays on the word it was placed on, even if you add or remove words before it. If you delete a linked word, its link goes too.
  • If you change a word that comes more than once, and tC4 can't tell which copy you kept, the verse is flagged for review. Open it in Align, check the links, and click ✓ Mark valid again.
  • A verse that needs review shows a notice at the top of the verse in Align and is marked in the verse list.
  • Making or breaking a verse span has the same effect on those verses.
  • A checking tool may reopen at the item you were on. Use the Invalid filter to find every item that needs review.
